## 摘要
离散扩散模型和流匹配已成为离散状态空间生成建模的强大框架，但高效的少步生成仍然是一个根本性的挑战。在这项工作中，我们引入了离散平均生成器（Discrete Average Generator），这是 MeanFlow 到连续时间马尔可夫链（CTMCs）的一种原则性扩展。类似于 MeanFlow 在连续空间中定义时间间隔内的平均速度场，我们定义了一个平均生成器作为时间间隔内转移核的归一化增量。我们证明了该平均生成器满足自洽性恒等式，这为我们的训练目标提供了基础。我们进一步开发了与扩散语言模型标准训练范式相一致的训练策略，同时保持了所得目标的易处理性。当投影到各坐标边缘分布上时，自洽性恒等式允许闭式表达，从而实现了高效的训练和推理。在 Potts 模型模拟中，我们的目标将 K 步采样器的总变差距离降低了高达 67%。在 OpenWebText 上，我们的方法在 8 到 64 个采样步数下实现了所评估方法中最低的生成困惑度，同时实现了 16 倍的加速，并在 ImageNet 上达到了与现有方法相当的性能。

## Abstract
Discrete diffusion models and flow matching have emerged as powerful frameworks for generative modeling over discrete state spaces, yet efficient few-step generation remains a fundamental challenge. In this work, we introduce the Discrete Average Generator, a principled extension of MeanFlow to Continuous-Time Markov Chains (CTMCs). Analogously to how MeanFlow defines an average velocity field over a time interval in continuous spaces, we define an average generator as the normalized increment of the transition kernel over a time interval. We show that this average generator satisfies a self-consistency identity, which provides the foundation for our training objective. We further develop training strategies that align with the standard training paradigm of diffusion language models while keeping the resulting objective tractable. When projected onto per-coordinate marginals, the self-consistency identity admits a closed-form expression, enabling efficient training and inference. In Potts model simulations, our objective reduces the total variation distance of the $K$-step sampler by up to 67%. On OpenWebText, our method achieves the lowest generative perplexity among the evaluated methods for 8 to 64 sampling steps while enabling a $16\times$ acceleration, and achieves comparable performance to existing methods on ImageNet.
