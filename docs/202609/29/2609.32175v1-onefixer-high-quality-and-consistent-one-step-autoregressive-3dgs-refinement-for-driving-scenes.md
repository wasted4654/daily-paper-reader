## 摘要
自回归视频扩散模型是自动驾驶仿真中 3D 高斯泼溅（3DGS）渲染修复的一种极具前景的方法，但其实际部署要求在低延迟下具备高视觉质量和时间一致性。这对单步因果生成而言尤为困难，因为每一个不完美的预测都会立即成为后续帧的上下文。现有方法通过多模块分阶段训练和滚动感知正则化来稳定滚动生成，但其单步生成质量仍难以满足部署需求。我们提出了 OneFixer，这是一种在单一任务特定适配阶段训练的单步自回归视频扩散修复器。我们的核心思想是采用部署匹配的共享滚动生成策略：模型自身的单步预测作为流匹配（flow matching）的因果上下文，使训练过程暴露于部署时的误差中，同时该滚动生成过程接收直接的像素空间感知监督以保留精细细节。由于针对当前帧质量优化的预测结果正是被复用为未来上下文的内容，因此保真度和自回归鲁棒性得以联合学习，无需进行双向到因果的转换或教师-学生蒸馏。OneFixer 进一步利用了自动驾驶仿真中易于获取的车道几何和动态智能体状态等线索，以提升几何保真度。在 Waymo 和包含 900 帧滚动生成的私有驾驶场景中，OneFixer 在单步生成下实现了所有基线模型中最低的 FVD、LPIPS 和 DISTS 指标，且时间一致性达到或超过了多阶段 DMD 流水线。在相同的骨干网络和条件设置下，它以不到一半的 GPU 耗时匹配了多阶段 DMD-with-Self-Forcing 流水线，并能在其性能瓶颈之外持续提升。在与驾驶策略的闭环仿真中，OneFixer 将碰撞率较原始 3DGS 渲染降低了三分之一。项目主页：https://onefixer-web.vercel.app/

## Abstract
Autoregressive video diffusion is a promising render-time fixer for 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) in autonomous-driving simulation, but deployment demands high visual quality and temporal consistency at low latency. This is especially hard for one-step causal generation, where each imperfect prediction immediately becomes context for subsequent frames. Existing approaches stabilize rollouts through staged training with multiple modules and rollout-aware regularization, yet one-step quality still falls short of what deployment requires. We introduce OneFixer, a one-step autoregressive video-diffusion fixer trained in a single task-specific adaptation stage. Our key idea is a deployment-matched shared rollout: the model's own one-step predictions serve as the causal context for flow matching, exposing training to deployment-time errors, while the same rollout receives direct pixel-space perceptual supervision to preserve fine detail. Because the predictions optimized for current-frame quality are exactly those reused as future context, fidelity and autoregressive robustness are learned jointly, without bidirectional-to-causal conversion or teacher-student distillation. OneFixer further exploits cues that driving simulation readily provides, lane geometry and dynamic-agent states, to improve geometric fidelity. On Waymo and proprietary driving scenes with 900-frame rollouts, OneFixer achieves the lowest FVD, LPIPS, and DISTS among all baselines at one step, with temporal consistency matching or exceeding multi-stage DMD pipelines. Under identical backbone and conditioning, it matches a multi-stage DMD-with-Self-Forcing pipeline in under half the GPU-hours and keeps improving beyond its plateau. In closed-loop simulation with a driving policy, OneFixer reduces the collision rate by a third relative to raw 3DGS rendering. Project page: https://onefixer-web.vercel.app/
